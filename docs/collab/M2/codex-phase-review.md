## Freeze blockers and concrete corrections

1. **Composite aging can double-credit income and double-age people.** phase-map step 2 says relations age_up, but Child.age_up credits annual net, can find a job, ages its partner and prompts; Parent/Partner routines can also contain more than core drift. Step 4 then proposes income for working children again. year_start increments player age while player_stats must not call Person.age_up, which increments it a second time. **Fix:** write exact headless entry points for age increment, stat drift and lifecycle effects; walk canonical living people once per turn with visited IDs. C extracts subrules, A calls only those. Mark for every phase which attributes/accounts it may mutate; forbid composite legacy age_up/random_events in the web coordinator. Snapshot starting people so newly born babies are age zero and don't get an extra annual update. Income/job-acquisition ordering must follow a documented rule for someone newly employed or retiring during the turn.

2. **Posting keys collide between accounts and repeated actions.** map's `{turn_id}:{source}` means player salary and every working child's salary share one key; the second account is silently dropped. Two paid treatments/lottery purchases in a year can also share a source. **Fix:** stable event/request/phase occurrence identity plus account ID and posting-leg ID, e.g. `{turn_id}:{phase}:{occurrence_id}:{account_id}:{leg}`. All legs of a transfer share a transaction/group ID but keep unique posting keys. Same key with changed amount/account must raise conflict rather than silently return an unrelated original entry. Persist replay ownership, posted keys and balances atomically.

3. **Cursor examples skip phases / cannot resume within a phase.** rule_result_example returns family_events cursor5 after a salary posting, skipping debt_service in the table. pending_choice_example says annual_events cursor8 although zero-based index8 is decision_offer. Returning annual_events as a continuation has no within-phase event cursor, so it can rerun the same divorce offer indefinitely. **Fix:** one representation `{phase_id, item_cursor, event_id?, step?}` with named phase-to-next mapping; don't duplicate phase name and numeric phase index without validation. Store continuation *after the current event*, sampled choice outcomes and per-entity completion markers. Provide examples for two choices in one annual phase, two different people, debt before family, and interruption after one account posting.

4. **Annual quote must be stable and not a preview RNG consumer.** C's existing Player.annual_income_breakdown samples expenses on every call and returns a tuple, while the map declares a person/year dict. **Fix:** agree pure deterministic arithmetic plus once-per-account/turn sampling. Store the immutable quote; project deterministic labeled previews without gameplay RNG. Choose integer rounding and prove posted gross−tax−expenses equals credited net; debt-service postings are additional and not already deducted. Example gross 30000/tax4200/expenses9000 is illustrative, not verified legacy tax; use the real helper's expected values in executable fixtures.

5. **Mortality and lifecycle ordering require an explicit parity decision.** Income before family/illness/player mortality can give full annual pay to someone who dies mid-sequence or can continue events after a fatal illness. M1 previously checked death before offering its contextual choice; moving mortality after decision_offer permits a decision before a pending death. **Fix:** declare whether time is end-of-year accounting and document ordering against reachable legacy rules. Fatal effects return an explicit terminal transition immediately; no later phases/choices/actions execute unless they are designated estate/close phases. Close the partial turn/ledger exactly once even on death. Decide whether deceased family members earn this period's income and where inheritance posts. Do not use `finished_turn` to skip necessary year-close persistence.

6. **M1 migration cursor is ambiguous.** “decision_offer or degenerate path” is not a frozen migration rule. M1 pending choices already followed a committed age increment/year effects; starting year_start or sampling again repeats them. Old M1 public p_XX references also need explicit UUID migration before new rules run. **Fix:** table of idle M1, pending M1 and dead M1 states → exact M2 cursor/status; backfill/remap all references and persisted replay snapshots coherently, without replaying already-committed effects. Test each case with real M1 envelope fixtures.

7. **No unapproved mechanics in example contracts.** apply_university example imposes an application fee of $50 not established by the inventory, and age_17_18 may not match the reachable legacy admission window. **Fix:** derive cost/age/qualifications from C's parity matrix, label placeholder values explicitly, and replace them before freeze. User requested parity, not incidental new balance changes from example JSON.
