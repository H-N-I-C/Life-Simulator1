# M20 — Optional storytelling director

User-approved addition, 2026-11-17. Read [delivery rules/dependencies](../M13-20-plan.md). Builds on M6 event chains, M13 history, M14 autonomous people, M15 motives, M18 content metadata and M19 reproducible comparisons.

## Outcome

Choose a pacing preference and experience coherent quiet stretches, developing tensions and occasional turning points. The director connects eligible content to established people, history and unresolved choices; it does not manufacture facts or override the simulation.

## Playable stages

- **M20a — Pacing controls:** choose calm, balanced or dramatic pacing, or turn the director off. See clear setting descriptions and retain a playable ordinary life in every mode.
- **M20b — Connected stories:** develop a relationship, project or recovery thread across years, with suitable follow-ups and room for unrelated everyday life. The player can decline/abandon a thread.
- **M20c — Long-life tuning:** compare matched/forked starts across pacing modes, inspect a spoiler-safe story recap and verify variety across ages, locations and generations.

## Content and model

Tag eligible events with narrative role, setup/payoff links, recency/cooldown, intensity, life-stage applicability and required established facts. Start with career reinvention, friendship repair, family support and creative-project threads using the real feature systems. Provide quiet follow-up scenes as well as dramatic outcomes.

Freeze a saved director state/budget, versioned weight policy, setting-change behavior, eligibility/explanation API and diagnostic counters. Start with a local deterministic selection policy using authored data; adaptive storytelling has no dependency on an external model service. Fixed historical occurrences and mandatory rules retain their semantics. Freeze any authored probability changes explicitly; do not dilute independent legacy gates by putting them back in a weighted flavor pool.

## Browser acceptance and exit checks

1. Play a short connected thread in each pacing mode and with the director off; turning it off preserves the world's underlying progression.
2. Reload a pending turning point: changing pacing cannot reroll its stored outcome or replace its bound person. Future selection changes only at defined boundaries.
3. Force scarce eligible content, repeated rejected invitations, unresolved threads and a recent major loss. No forced trauma loop, mandatory relationship repair or inaccessible free exit.
4. Prove eligibility runs before weighting: no underage romance, manufactured consent, reaction to an unknown secret, nonexistent job/relative, unpayable mandatory choice or duplicated consequence.
5. Save/restore pacing state through succession and branch comparisons; same state/actions/settings reproduce selection. Measure repetition and setup/payoff completion rather than maximizing event count.

## Traps and ownership

Do not confuse pacing with changing the player's history, guarantee a success/failure because it makes a convenient story, or turn dramatic mode into repeated bereavement. Explanations must not leak future samples/private NPC motives. One owner delivers selection/content metadata/settings/recap UI end to end; domain owners expose eligible hooks. D runs contrasting lives and regression checks on mandatory event probabilities.
