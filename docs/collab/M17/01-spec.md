# M17 — Creative works with a lasting life

User-approved addition, 2026-11-17. Read [delivery rules/dependencies](../M13-20-plan.md). Extends M6 projects, M8 legacy, M11 audiences and M14 collaborators. Earlier milestones can complete a project; this milestone makes its output a persistent, inspectable work.

## Outcome

A novel, album or film has a name, genre, credited people, production history, reception and an audience that can outlive its creator. Descendants can discover a relative's work rather than seeing only a generic completed-project badge.

## Playable stages

- **M17a — Make and keep a work:** finish a novel, choose a title/genre and release privately or publicly. Open its artifact page, inspect credits and keep it in the family archive.
- **M17b — Release and reception:** produce an album or film with collaborators, compare release routes/budgets, receive original fictional reviews and track bounded audience/revenue changes.
- **M17c — Afterlife of a work:** earn ongoing receipts, experience renewed interest in an older work, transfer rights through existing inheritance and discover the artifact while playing a descendant.

## Content and model

Provide a complete authoring/release/reception loop for each of novels, albums and films, reusing existing projects and specialist skills. Include small private/hobby releases as well as commercial success, mixed reviews and overlooked work. Generate inspectable summaries/credits/reviews from authored templates and saved outcomes; ordinary play works locally with no external generation service required.

Freeze work IDs, authorship/credit roles, project linkage, release versions, rights ownership, reception occurrences and royalty/payment contracts. Distinguish production costs, sales receipts, collaborator shares and ownership. Artifact exports include original game text and respect known/public information.

## Browser acceptance and exit checks

1. Complete and open a named work through normal project actions; reload/export preserves its title, credits and sampled reception.
2. Produce a collaborative release: reconcile budget, revenue shares and recurring receipts through the existing ledger exactly once per period.
3. Rename or lose a collaborator without breaking historical credits/person references. A creator's death preserves the work and transfers rights according to the recorded arrangement.
4. Continue as a descendant, find the artifact and encounter a plausible renewed-interest event without manufacturing earlier fame or reviews.
5. Test free/private releases, failed projects, duplicate submissions and multiple editions. Releasing a work does not automatically confer fame or guaranteed profits.

## Traps and ownership

Do not replace a completed work with randomized text every time its page opens, credit people by mutable names, or pay the same receipts through both project and fame systems. Separate physical keepsakes from intellectual rights. One feature owner ships the artifact UI, release rules/content and tests; domain adapters connect existing project/fame/estate systems. D checks three complete creative journeys and multigeneration continuity.
