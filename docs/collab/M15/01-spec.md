# M15 — An inner life

User-approved addition, 2026-11-17. Read [delivery rules/dependencies](../M13-20-plan.md). Builds on M6 commitments/projects, M8 goals and M14 persistent NPC lives. Extends static traits and external achievements with evolving personal priorities.

## Outcome

Characters have values, ambitions, insecurities and changing priorities. Wealth, fame, happiness and fulfillment can differ. A character may pursue an inherited ambition, reconsider it, start again late in life or find satisfaction in an ordinary routine.

## Playable stages

- **M15a — Priorities and fulfillment:** inspect a readable personal-priorities panel, choose an ambition, and see how work/family/leisure commitments support or strain it. Show understandable reasons for changes.
- **M15b — Change of direction:** play a multiyear conflict between priorities, reflect after an established event, and revise an ambition with ongoing effects rather than an instant stat reset.
- **M15c — Motives in relationships:** learn an NPC's priorities through interaction; support or clash with them. In the next generation, family expectations influence a child without dictating their identity or ambition.

## Content and model

Initial priority families: belonging/care, autonomy/exploration, mastery/creativity and security/recognition. Starter chains include career disillusionment, a return to learning, reconciliation with family expectations and a fulfilling modest life. Avoid a single universal best personality or a compulsory wealth/fame victory.

Freeze versioned motive profiles, active ambitions, fulfillment explanations, reflection triggers and knowledge/editor projections. Existing health/happiness remain distinct systems. Motives modify choices and bounded outcomes, never override protected consent/18+ or turn adult preferences into hard bans. Relevant editor changes follow the established saved-offer supersession policy without rerolling consent.

## Browser acceptance and exit checks

1. Play two matched starts with different priorities: choose comparable opportunities and inspect different fulfillment consequences, without changing their objective salary/qualification rules.
2. Complete a reflection chain across reloads and revise an ambition once; preserve previous milestones and costs.
3. Observe an NPC's motive-driven behavior before and after disclosure. Private motives do not leak through raw state/reason payloads.
4. Test conflicting priorities, low-cash and low-stat starts, elder goals and succession. Ordinary care/leisure paths can sustain fulfillment.
5. Assert bounded values, actual clamped journal deltas, once-only reflection triggers and deterministic outcomes. Migration preserves existing traits/goals without inventing past crises.

## Traps and ownership

Do not reduce every feeling to another grindable happiness bar, equate financial hardship with personal failure, or infer values from gender, appearance or ancestry. Avoid silently editing historical motives after a goal changes. One feature owner delivers motive rules, reflection content and UI; shared goal/knowledge/editor adapters remain explicit. D checks contrasting lives and information visibility.
